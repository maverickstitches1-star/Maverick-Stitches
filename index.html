MEASUREMENT GUIDE

Take Accurate Measurements Like a Professional

Follow this guide to ensure your custom outfit fits perfectly.

Neck

Measure around the base of your neck comfortably. Leave one finger space inside the tape.

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest while standing naturally.

Shoulder

Measure from one shoulder edge to the other across your upper back.

Sleeve Length

Measure from your shoulder to your wrist with your arm slightly bent.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline without holding your breath.

Trouser Length

Measure from the waist down to your desired trouser length.
